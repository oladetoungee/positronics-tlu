We're three master's students in Human-Computer Interaction at Tallinn University.

What this is about

We're working with Positronics OÜ, the company behind Positron Flux, to understand how engineering leaders read and act on the insights the product finds about how a team's behavioral metrics link to the objectives its leader sets. What we learn will shape our designs and our course report.

What you'll do

The session takes about 30 to 45 minutes on a video call.

  1. We'll ask about your teams, the objectives you set, and how you check on how they're doing.
  2. If you agree, you'll share your screen and show us what you use today. This is optional.
  3. You'll use Positron Flux yourself, through a temporary invitation to Positronics' own account, and complete three short tasks.
  4. We'll show you some cards about an insight and ask what you make of them, and how much you'd trust them.

There are no right or wrong answers. We're testing the product, not you, and we want to hear about your own experience.

Your data

  • Recording. With your agreement, we record the audio and video of the call. If you share your screen, we only record it if you say yes below.
  • Anonymity. We give you a code, such as P03. Your name and your company's name never appear in anything we write. When we quote you, we take out anything that could identify you or your company.
  • Who sees it. Only the three of us see the recordings and notes. Our teachers and Positronics see only anonymised findings and quotes.
  • Storage. Recordings and notes are kept in a private Tallinn University drive, never in public places.
  • Deletion. All recordings and notes are deleted at the end of the course, by 31 December 2026.
  • Your company's information. Please don't share anything confidential about your company. If you'd rather not show something, just describe it.

Keeping Positron Flux confidential

Positron Flux isn't public yet. Please don't share, screenshot or describe outside this session anything you see of the product or its data.

Your rights

Taking part is voluntary, and there's no payment. You can skip any question, pause, or stop at any time without giving a reason, and nothing bad comes of it. You can ask us to delete your data at any time until 31 December 2026, by writing to gbemigee@tlu.ee.

There may be no direct benefit to you. What you tell us helps make Positron Flux's insights easier to understand and to trust.
